# Figure Patterns — counts, role grammar, maps, timelines, captions, tables

consolidated: 2026-08-26, N=60
Tiers: corpus-consensus >=30/60; common >=12/60; variant <12 multi-doc; outlier single-doc.
Counts from benchmark-stats.json: figures/article min=4, median=6, max=7 (p25=5, p75=6).

## Count and format [corpus-consensus]
- 4-7 numbered figures per article; median 6; mode 6 in newest batch. (index-wide; B021-B030 10/10;
  B041-B050 10/10; B051-B060)
- Nearly all figures are multi-panel with lettered panels (a-f), cited inline as "(Fig. 2a)".
  (B021-B030 10/10; B051-B060 >=7/10; B041-B050 8/10 mid-paper montages)
- Tables are scarce (0-2), reserved for quantitative consolidation; boxes carry side material.
  (B041-B050 convention; response-matrix variants B032,B034,B037,B040)

## Role grammar of the figure sequence [corpus-consensus as family]
1. Fig 1 = orientation device: conceptual framework schematic, taxonomy, process diagram, or
   genealogy that names the vocabulary later sections use. (~34/60: B003,B005,B007,B009,B010,
   B011,B016-B019,B026,B027,B029,B030,B031-B034,B036,B037,B039,B040,B041-B050 10/10,B051-B060 9/10)
2. Mid-paper multi-panel evidence figures: time series with ensemble bands, maps, threshold charts,
   effect-size compilations. (most docs; B021-B030 meta-displays 5/10; B041-B050 8/10)
3. Late synthesis figure consolidating state, cascades, feedback loops, or systemic gaps.
   (B023 Fig7 gaps diagram,B025 Fig6 roadmap,B030 Fig5 matrix,B041,B042,B046,B047,B049,B050; ~10/60)
   [common]

## Maps as evidence currency [corpus-consensus, ~31/60]
Maps wherever distribution is evidential: climatology/trends, event impacts, coverage density,
counterfactual scenario geographies, trade-flow arrows, hotspot catalogues with direction-of-change
annotations. (B001,B002,B005,B006,B007,B009,B010,B011,B013,B015,B016,B017,B019,B020,B021,B023 x4,
B025,B026,B027,B029,B030,B041,B042,B045,B047,B048,B049 + batch-6 map grammars)

## Figure grammars mapped to rhetorical jobs [each grammar variant-to-common]
- Timeline/milestone figures: field maturation, system evolution, dated event matrices; one doc
  interleaves science+policy milestones. (B012,B021,B023,B025,B028,B043,B051,B057,B058,B059 Box)
- Typology figures when equifinality threatens interpretation (different processes -> similar
  signature). (B052) [outlier-as-type]
- Workflow/procedure diagrams documenting assessed protocols. (B012,B051,B057) [variant]
- Observed+projected fused panel sets with scenario fans. (B001,B002,B006,B031,B033,B036,B038,B039) [common]
- Evidence-audit figures grading sub-topics by literature coverage/severity/confidence.
  (B055,B023 study-distribution map) [variant]

## Caption norms [consensus/common split]
- Self-contained captions declaring data sources, thresholds, controls, panel logic so figures
  stand alone. (B021-B030 10/10; corpus expectation) [corpus-consensus]
- Caption-as-finding: final sentence states the takeaway message, not just contents; explicit
  data-source/threshold statements included. (B013,B019,B032,B036,B039,B042,B045,B046,B048,B050-
  style,B051-B055,B058; ~14+/60) [common]
- Epistemic status encoded graphically AND declared: dashed arrows = high-uncertainty links;
  shading = confidence ranges; thick lines = ensemble means; +/- marks flag records; qualitative
  classification criteria disclosed. (B001,B006,B007,B009) [common pooled]

## Evidence-summary tables [common, ~14/60]
Tables compiling many studies into checkable rows with per-row provenance and honest cells
(including explicit "Unknown globally"); method-x-certainty matrices; scale taxonomies; study-precis
matrices. (B002,B005,B006,B010,B012,B016,B019,B030,B032,B034,B037,B040,B043,B046,B050,B053,B054,B057)

## Scale-ladder and state-space devices [variant]
Scale matrices making spatial/temporal resolution choices explicit; two-dimensional state-space
diagrams linking two measurement domains. (B023,B024,B010 Fig4b-style,B002 Fig2-style)
