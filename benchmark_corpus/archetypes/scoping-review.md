# Archetype: Scoping Review

consolidated: 2026-08-26, N=60
Corpus coverage: NONE — zero of 60 documents are scoping reviews. No card carries a scoping label;
no digest shows a gap-map or charting-table apparatus. This file therefore describes the archetype
generically for runtime contrast, and flags that none of its frequency claims are corpus-derived.

## Generic signature architecture (NOT corpus-calibrated)
- Research-question mapping breadth over depth: what IS known, by whom, where, with which methods —
  not what is TRUE.
- Protocol-transparent identification stage (database searches, inclusion/exclusion screens,
  PRISMA-style flow accounting of records).
- Body organized by concept clusters / population-context categories rather than causal ladders;
  charting tables summarizing study characteristics per cluster.
- Outputs are maps of the literature: evidence atlases, coverage matrices, identified-heterogeneity
  catalogues; quality appraisal deliberately omitted or minimal.

## Generic signature rhetoric (NOT corpus-calibrated)
- Descriptive-analytic register: frequencies of study attributes ("N studies, mostly X, concentrated
  in Y") rather than proposition adjudication.
- Gaps stated as empty cells of the mapping grid, with recommendations for WHERE future primary
  research should go.

scoping form.

## Runtime guidance
When a user requests a scoping review, do NOT import NREE house conventions wholesale (proposition-
led clustering, funnel ladders). Keep breadth-first mapping, screen accounting, and charting tables;
borrow only the corpus's caption craft, roadmap fidelity, and honest "Unknown" cells, which transfer
