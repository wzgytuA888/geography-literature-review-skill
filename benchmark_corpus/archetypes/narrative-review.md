# Archetype: Narrative Review

consolidated: 2026-08-26, N=60
Corpus coverage: STRONG — the largest card-labelled cohort (~22/60: B001,B004,B006,B007,B009,B011,
B015,B016,B019,B020 plus annotated narrative variants B047,B058 and most early-batch docs).
Note honestly: all corpus narratives live in one journal house style; pure "essay-style" narrative
with no fixed skeleton is NOT represented.

## Signature architecture
- Fixed skeleton: Abstract -> framing Introduction -> 3-6 thematic body sections -> terminal
  "Summary and future perspectives". (corpus-consensus; see review-architecture.md)
- Body order is an explicit progression ladder (causal funnel, temporal eras, or scale ladder), not
  a topic list.
- Boxes quarantine definitions/methods/derivations; flat heading hierarchy with run-in mini-
  headings for depth.

## Signature rhetoric
- Five-move introduction ending in a sequential roadmap with roadmap fidelity to delivered sections.
- Proposition-led paragraphs with citation clusters; zero study-by-study narration.
- Triangulation across >=2 independent evidence streams before key claims; contradiction handled by
  source-diagnosis then complementarity or subdivision (see synthesis-patterns.md ladder).
- Quantified exemplars: claim -> place + number + period + citation.
- Expert-judgement register reserved for gaps/agenda (uncited by design).

## Evidence apparatus
- Maps as evidence currency; Fig 1 conceptual orientation device; self-contained captions with
  takeaway final sentences.
- Reference profile: median ~165 refs; recent-5y share median ~0.24 rising with field speed;
  seminal anchors carry theory while recent work carries projections/attribution.

## Imitation risks (from cards)
Theory-first ordering loses readers without pedagogy scaffolding (B001); proposing new metrics
requires genuine formal work behind them (B047); era partitioning risks repetition when processes
span windows (B058).
