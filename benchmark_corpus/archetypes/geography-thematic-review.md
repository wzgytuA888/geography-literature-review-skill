# Archetype: Geography-Thematic Review

consolidated: 2026-08-26, N=60
Corpus coverage: DOMINANT — the de-facto mode of the corpus. ~23/60 cards carry explicit thematic/
thematic-mixed labels and most narrative-labelled docs are thematically organized too; effectively
~45+/60 execute this archetype in one journal house style. This is the corpus's centre of gravity.

## Signature architecture
- Full house skeleton: Abstract -> Introduction -> 3-6 named thematic body sections -> terminal
  Summary-and-future. (corpus-consensus)
- Body order = progression ladder matched to the phenomenon's own structure: causal funnel
  (drivers->observations->projections->responses), temporal eras, spatial scale ladders, or spatial-
  position organization (upstream->downstream, elevation bands). (see review-architecture.md)
- Parallel per-class/per-region subsection templates with mirrored move orders.
- Human-environment coupling built into the spine: management/policy sections close the paper as
  first-class components, not appendices. (~28/60 human-footprint rule)

## Signature rhetoric
- Five-move quantified introduction with roadmap fidelity.
- Regional differentiation ONLY under evidence conditions (R1 contract): every place contrast
  carries its why; places anchor magnitudes but never organize sections — except single-place deep
  syntheses where internal geography IS the analysis (B023 delta; B058 ice sheet).
- Scale-dependence stated at every scale shift; cross-scale cautions on upscaling.
- Flows between places (rivers, trade, teleconnections) used to connect regions into systems.
- Policy documents, water law, markets treated as analysable data inside the synthesis.

## Evidence apparatus
- Maps-as-evidence wherever distribution matters; Fig 1 conceptual orientation schematic; multi-
  panel lettered evidence montages; timeline figures for system evolution; gaps diagrams distilled
  from coverage audits.
- Evidence-summary tables with provenance columns and honest Unknown cells.
- Figure count 4-7 (median 6).

regionalism breaking the R1 contract (A2/B054 counter-example note); parallel templates turning
mechanical when sibling sections lack distinct mechanisms (A19/B046 risk).
