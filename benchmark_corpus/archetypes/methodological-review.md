# Archetype: Methodological Review

consolidated: 2026-08-26, N=60
Corpus coverage: MODERATE — 5/60 card-labelled methodological docs (B012 assessment-framework
methodological history; B035 worked-example methods review; B057 method-pipeline review with
running-case pedagogy; plus two further methodological-labelled cards incl. a metric-comparison and
an evidence-audit/attribution doc). All are method reviews fused with thematic content; pure
methods-taxonomy papers without a phenomenon are absent.

## Signature architecture
- House skeleton retained, but body sections follow a METHOD PIPELINE (acquisition -> processing ->
  interpretation -> reporting) or an option-space taxonomy rather than a phenomenon ladder.
  (B057 pipeline exception noted in batch-6; B010 metric-by-metric mirrored survey)
- Model-evaluation gate: a dedicated section/Box states selection criteria and validation evidence
  BEFORE any results or recommendations. (B039 model-skill gate; B038 methods-audit section;
  B027 remote-sensing product audit run-in)
- Worked examples inside Boxes/figures demonstrating the method step by step. (B035 walkthrough;
  B057 running-case figures)

## Signature rhetoric
- Criteria tables comparing options across stated dimensions (accuracy, cost, coverage, assumptions).
  (B057 application taxonomy; B053 estimate table by timescale variant)
- Framing-sensitivity demonstrated explicitly: same data under different definitions produce
  different verdicts, shown not asserted. (B057)
- Standards/protocols cited as primary evidence objects by code. (B012 norms; B019 assessment
  reports as data)
- Reflexive disclosure of author position relative to the assessed framework. (B012 candidate)

## Evidence apparatus
- Workflow/flowchart figures documenting assessed protocols. (B012,B051,B057)
- Evolution-timeline figures spanning framework/report generations. (B012,B043 genealogy variant)
- Comparative-evidence tables consolidating contested quantities outside prose. (B053,B054,B057)
- Dataset/version citations at first mention with limitations; supplementary methods pointers.
  (B036,B037,B041 supplement pattern)

## Exemplar doc IDs
B010, B012, B035, B057, plus audit-heavy variants B038, B039.

## Imitation risks (from cards)
Method-catalogue sections degenerate into lists without a connecting evaluation criterion chain
(A19); insider frameworks need external uptake evidence (B043); low citation density is legitimate
only when the unit is a concept/method step, never for empirical claims (batch-6 candidate note).
