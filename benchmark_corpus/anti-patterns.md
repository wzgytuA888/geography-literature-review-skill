# Anti-Patterns — moves these 60 benchmark reviews avoid (runtime avoid-list)

consolidated: 2026-08-26, N=60
Tiers describe ABSENCE evidence: corpus-consensus absence = avoided in >=30/60 scoreable docs;
common absence >=12/60; variant absence <12; risk = miner-flagged imitation hazard.
Presence of any item below in a draft is a defect signal.

## A1. Study-by-study enumeration [corpus-consensus absence]
"Author A found ... Author B found ..." paragraph chains, or single-source paragraphs carrying
general claims. Exemplars appear only as "For example," instantiations AFTER a governing claim.
(batch-wide low enumeration ratios; B001-B060 readable set)

## A2. Decorative regionalism [corpus-consensus absence]
Naming regions without an evidence condition for why setting matters. Every place-based contrast
arrives with its mechanism. (see geography-reasoning-patterns.md R1)

## A3. Single-stream attribution for key claims [common absence]
Precision substituted for independence; consensus claimed from one evidence stream instead of
triangulation. (B002,B006,B007,B010 discipline)

## A4. Undisclosed definitional arbitrariness [common absence]
Thresholds/metrics presented as natural rather than chosen; definitional contention left inside
analytic prose instead of quarantined in Boxes/Glossaries. (B001,B010,B033,B035-B038 pattern)

## A5. Citing datasets without bias/provenance notes [variant-to-common absence]
Datasets used without version/vintage/known-limitation attachment at first mention.
(B006,B007,B013,B017,B019,B020 audit convention)

## A6. Hiding unusable data [outlier-documented absence]
Dropping estimates silently instead of exclusion-with-stated-reason; synthesis tables without
honest "Unknown" cells. (B010 exclusion note; B005 Unknown cells)

## A7. Short-record / unrepresentative trend inference [variant absence]
Trends from short or unrepresentative records; linear fits ignoring autocorrelation;
uncritical space-for-time substitution. (B016 stilling-type rebuttal register; B017 explicit flag)

## A8. Winner-picking among co-acting mechanisms [variant absence]
Adjudicating to a single winner without complementarity framing when mechanisms superpose.
(B011,B015,B018 counterexample discipline)

## A9. Undiagnosed disagreement [common absence]
Reporting spread or contradiction without naming its source (metric/method/resolution/missing
process). (B021,B026,B028 guard encoded; B052,B058 positive pattern)

## A10. Unsituated numbers [batch-consensus absence]
Statistics quoted without place, period and source adjacent; silent averaging of incompatible
metrics. (B045,B049 display-both rule; batch-5 10/10 situating convention)

## A11. Point totals without intervals [variant absence]
Single-point global totals with no range, error, n, or provenance disclosure. (B025,B030 discipline)

## A12. Unhedged superlatives; mislocated hedges [batch-absence]
Superlatives without quantified anchors; hedging placed away from the thin-evidence claim it
belongs to. (batch-4 10/10 absence judgement)

## A13. Forcing heterogeneous responses into one trend [common absence]
Mixed/null results erased instead of kept as an explicit third category. (B031,B032,B037,B038)

## A14. Projections from unscreened model pools [variant absence]
Consensus claimed over model ensembles without skill screening or stated selection criteria.
(B033,B036,B039 gate-first pattern)

## A15. Undefined term drift [variant absence]
Key terms shifting meaning across sections; disambiguation deferred past first use.
(B043,B046,B047 once-early rule)

## A16. Unsupported neglect/gap claims [consensus-family absence]
"Little studied" assertions without demonstrated deficits; gaps asserted before the evidence audit
that justifies them. (see gap-identification-patterns.md)

## A17. Unexplained scale jumps [variant absence]
Shifts between site/region/globe without ladders, matrices, or framework figures to signpost them.
(B042,B047,B049 signposting convention)

## A18. Scope-only abstracts [batch-consensus absence]
Abstracts announcing scope without concrete findings or numbers. (batch-6 10/10 sampled abstracts
carry results)

## A19. Catalogue degeneration [risk, flagged on cards]
Parallel per-category sections degrading into a list with no connecting requirement/mechanism
chain; region-by-region prose with no cross-cutting driver synthesis afterward. (B022,B028,B040,B046 risks)

## A20. Advocacy outrunning evidence stratification [risk]
Call-to-action framing copied without the confidence-grading layer that keeps it calibrated.
(B024,B048 risk notes)

## A21. Uniform prescriptions across divergent regions [counter-example encoded]
Global one-size recommendations where drivers are regional; corpus performs region-specific logic.
(B054 explicit opposite)
