# Gap Identification Patterns — derived vs asserted gaps, quantified deficits

consolidated: 2026-08-26, N=60
Tiers: corpus-consensus >=30/60; common >=12/60; variant <12 multi-doc; outlier single-doc.

## Master rule: gaps are DERIVED from demonstrated failure, never asserted [corpus-consensus, ~38/60]
A gap claim must be preceded, inside the review body, by a shown deficit or mismatch:
- policy conclusions precluded by quantified uncertainty (B007);
- hazard-trend vs impact-trend mismatch (B009);
- tools misperforming in the understudied case (B004);
- residual spread between independent methods (B006,B039);
- inability to date past events with current archives (B003);
- metric divergence (B010);
- extrapolation risk into untested regimes (B002).

## Evidence-audit devices that generate gaps [family consensus; devices individually common]
- Study-distribution maps turning coverage itself into an argument; uneven counts distilled into a
  dedicated gaps figure (two-stage derivation). (B023 Fig2+Fig7 pattern)
- Empty cells of assembled observation matrices "speak" as gaps. (B050)
- Satellite/measured extent compared against recorded coverage to quantify the unmeasured share.
  (B048)
- Record-length, depth-coverage and network-sparsity audits before trend trust. (B011,B015,B016,
  B024,B027)
- Cited inventories shown to omit the review's class. (B041)
- Effectiveness literature surveyed then declared absent for key interventions. (B042 fishway-type
  cases,B044,B046)
- Estimate-divergence demonstrated before standardisation calls. (B045)

## Quantified deficit anchors [common, ~12/60]
Gaps anchored to deficit statistics ("<10% of rivers gauged"-class numbers), n-count imbalances,
country-count asymmetries, authoritative assessment contrasts. (B031,B037,B038,B048)

## Gap type taxonomy [observed across batches; each type common-or-variant pooled]
1. Observational/monitoring coverage gaps (B011,B015,B016,B031,B036,B037,B039,B040).
2. Tool/model integration-coupling gaps (B011,B013,B018,B019,B034,B035,B037,B038).
3. Geographic coverage/equity gaps (B028 archive-rich vs archive-poor continents,B032,B037,B038,B040).
4. Measurement/method fragmentation gaps (B002 metric inconsistency,B045,B046,B047).
5. Intervention-efficacy unknowns (B042,B044,B046).
6. Absence-of-review/inventory gaps (B041,B048,B051,B052).
7. Detection-vs-attribution separation needs (B016,B019,B020).
8. Silo-crossing/disciplinary-integration gaps (B021,B024,B026,B030,B043).

## Asserted-gap register exists but is quarantined [variant-to-common, ~10/60 documented]
Capability voids appear uncited ONLY inside agenda/closing sections (author-judgement register),
never as analytic body claims; prior partial reviews are cited solely to prove an absence.
(B001,B007,B010,B021,B022,B024,B026,B051)

## Disagreement-to-gap conversion [variant, ~6/60]
Inconsistency among estimates attributed to a named cause, then restated as a knowledge gap:
"inconsistency hints at unconsolidated understanding" register; model spread decomposed process-
by-process then converted into targeted community vehicles. (B052,B058,B057 framing-sensitivity)

## Falsifiable-question conversion [variant, 3-6/60]
Unresolved disputes restated as explicitly testable questions or proposed threshold ranges rather
than open-ended wishes. (B025 trajectory question,B026 buffering question,B027 threshold ranges)

## Regulatory/operational gaps as research outcomes [outlier-to-variant, 2/60]
Absent reporting standards, unregulated practices, exemption lists treated as first-class findings.
(B055,B057)

## Anti-pattern guarded here
"Little studied" assertions without demonstrated deficits are absent from the corpus; unsupported
neglect claims are an anti-pattern (see anti-patterns.md item 18).
