# Synthesis Patterns — clustering, quantified backbones, triangulation, contradiction ladder

consolidated: 2026-08-26, N=60
Tiers: corpus-consensus >=30/60; common >=12/60; variant <12 multi-doc; outlier single-doc.

## Proposition-led clustering [corpus-consensus, ~55/60]
Citations attach to propositions; one quantified claim is supported by a multi-reference cluster;
papers are never the unit of narration. (B001,B003,B005,B006,B007,B009,B010 + all readable docs
in B011-B060; study-by-study ratio low everywhere except one medium outlier B040)

## Quantified synthesis backbone (review-as-data-analysis) [common, ~14/60]
Authors recompute pooled quantities, budgets, bounds or effect sizes from literature values and
disclose procedure (n, dispersion, derivation pointer, supplements).
(B017 own compilation 120 studies/B023 427-study pooling/B025 interval bounds/B027 pooled slopes/
B029 meta-effect sizes with n/B030 computed budgets/B036 pooled ensemble mean±spread/B037 tallies
with directional percentages/B038 sample-size-reported pooling/B039 fraction-of-models voting)
Discipline: each new number carries n, dispersion, and derivation location; recomputation without
this rigour is flagged as an imitation risk. (B025,B027,B029,B030 discipline notes)

## Triangulation before key claims [corpus-consensus, ~32/60]
Consensus claims rest on >=2 independent evidence streams (empirical+experimental+modelling;
multi-model generations; multiple satellite products; proxies vs models), with explicit
convergence clauses tying the verdict to independence. (B002,B006,B007,B009,B010,B021,B023,B025,
B027,B029,B047 "strong consensus" tied to metric+ensemble agreement,B049 all-estimations clause,
B052 cross-region convergence clause)

## Contradiction-handling ladder (ordered; never silent winner-picking)
Ladder step 1 — Diagnose the source: disagreement attributed to metric choice, model class,
resolution, definitions, or a named missing process. (B001,B006,B010,B015,B016,B019,B020,B021,
B023,B026,B028,B030,B033,B036,B039,B040,B052,B058; ~25/60) [corpus-consensus]
Step 2 — Reframe as complementary/superposed mechanisms acting in concert. (B011,B015,B018) [variant-to-common]
Step 3 — Reconcile by subdivision or scale: split mechanisms/traits/positions/definitions so both
findings coexist; scale-dependence as the reconciliation device. (B021,B023,B026,B029,B041,B042,
B043,B047; ~10/60) [common pooled with step 1 variants]
Step 4 — Display side-by-side with provenance instead of averaging incompatible estimates.
(B045 ERA-vs-alternate display,B049 dataset columns,B053 obs-vs-model table,B058 model-spread
decomposition) [common]
Step 5 — Convert divergence into numbered candidate explanations, each mapped to a research
action. (B047 First/Second/Third; B058 process-gap itemization) [variant]
Complement: enumerate-competing-explanations-then-converge on the robust shared mechanism.
("regardless of the exact trigger" register; B023,B028,B029) [common]

## Interval reporting over point estimates [common, ~16/60]
Synthesized quantities carry upper/lower bounds, mean±SD with n, %± error, or order-of-magnitude
declarations when precision is unavailable; aggregation sites carry explicit precision
disclaimers. (B001,B005,B006,B010,B023,B025,B029,B030,B052,B053,B058)

## Consensus language calibration [common, ~18/60]
Convergence phrases are tied to named independent sources ("consistent with", "together these
approaches reveal", "all evidence points towards"); strength graded by scope. (B001,B006,B007,
B010,B012,B015,B016,B020,B047,B049,B052)

## Mechanism-share attribution [variant, 2-4/60]
Competing explanations expressed as percentage contributions or variance rankings rather than
verbal weightings. (B056,B058)
