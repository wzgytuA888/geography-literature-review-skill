# Argument Patterns — claim-evidence relations, comparison, boundary conditions, weighting

consolidated: 2026-08-26, N=60
Tiers: corpus-consensus >=30/60; common >=12/60; variant <12 multi-doc; outlier single-doc.

## Claim-evidence relations
- Quantitative claim -> citation cluster -> provenance (source, period, accounting method adjacent
  to the number). (B045 strictest exemplar; B046,B048,B049 same convention; batch-4 6/10
  uncertainty-at-claim-sites) [common-to-consensus family]
- Null-model comparison argument: derive expectation from theory -> measure observed departure ->
  attribute departure to mechanism. (B001 statistical records; B047 expected-vs-observed;
  B006 counterfactual budget) [variant as full form; expectation-test logic recurring]
- Expected-vs-observed contrast template with secondary-driver rescue of the discrepancy.
  (B011,B014,B016) [variant]
- Dated event/case anchoring gives impact claims concreteness (named event + year + measured
  consequence). (B001-B004,B031-B034,B037,B039,B040; ~10/60 in batch-4 alone) [common]
- Framework-slot filling: every study positioned into exactly one named-framework slot, then slots
  connected. (B023 DPSIR-like) [outlier]

## Comparison patterns
- Mirrored contrasts sharpen interpretation: hot/cold, land/ocean, wet/dry basins, with/without
  reservoir, thermodynamic-vs-pluvial control. (B001,B003 sub-regions,B031,B036,B037,B042,B044) [common]
- Scenario-vs-scenario projection fans and warming-level exceedance displays. (B001,B002,B006) [variant]
- Winners-vs-losers regional contrast under identical forcing. (B013,B019) [variant]
- Paired-case comparison designed to adjudicate between competing explanations. (B034,B040,B028 deep-dive box variant) [variant]
- Era-vs-era budget comparisons across declared time windows. (B053,B058) [variant]
- Cross-biome/cross-region benchmarking positioning the studied system against peer regions.
  (B026,B027) [variant]

## Boundary-condition handling
- Conditions attached at claim sites: antecedent state, elevation zone, surface type, basin
  wetness modulate the stated response. (B047,B050,B058,B041 weak-unit extent,B042 upstream status) [common]
- Generalizability guards on flagship cases ("how general these effects are is unclear").
  (B015,B017) [variant]
- Definitional arbitrariness disclosed rather than hidden ("somewhat arbitrary and application-
  specific" register). (B001,B010) [common pooled]
- Mixture/heavy-tail or edge cases handled as extensions that preserve core validity. (B001) [variant]

## Evidence weighting (declared hierarchies)
- Gauge/in-situ observations over reanalysis; peer-reviewed estimates over grey literature;
  ensemble spread shown graphically rather than verbally only. (B045,B047,B049) [variant explicit;
  implicit elsewhere]
- Multimodel means over single runs; theoretical expectation as universal yardstick. (B001) [variant]
- Convergence across independent reconstructions outweighs any single estimate. (B023) [variant]
- Model-based reviews gate projections on demonstrated simulation skill and state selection
  criteria BEFORE presenting results. (B033,B036,B039) [common as principle in model-heavy reviews]
- Observations anchor, reanalyses bridge, models project — roles assigned per evidence class.
  (B058) [variant]

## Mixed/null results discipline [common, ~12/60]
Mixed, null and non-significant results retained as an explicit third category instead of being
forced into a trend; displayed with significance masks or power caveats. (B027 significance masks,
B029 power caveat,B031 inline exceptions,B032 exacerbate-or-compensate,B037 increase/decrease/
mixed legend,B038 dual-outcome statements)

## Sign-variation explanation [variant, 5/60]
Opposite-signed responses explained via a named mechanism dichotomy (e.g. dilution-vs-mobilization
logic classes) rather than left as scatter. (B031,B036,B037)
