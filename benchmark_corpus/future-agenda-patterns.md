# Future Agenda Patterns — imperative style, lever grouping, numbered priorities, closing moves

consolidated: 2026-08-26, N=60
Tiers: corpus-consensus >=30/60; common >=12/60; variant <12 multi-doc; outlier single-doc.

## Imperative + agent + instrument style [corpus-consensus, ~35/60]
Agenda items are phrased as imperative capability needs with a named agent and a concrete
instrument/platform/framework — never bare "more research needed" (no captured closing ends on a
vague call; batch-5 judged 10/10 closings).
(B001,B002,B003,B004,B007,B009,B016,B018,B019,B020,B021,B024,B025,B030,B031-B040 every Summary,
B041,B044,B047,B048,B049,B051,B055,B056,B058)
Template: VERB + target capability + named vehicle ("research initiatives need to archive output
at relevant heights" register).

## Lever grouping [common, ~14/60]
Agenda items grouped by lever class: observation networks, data sharing/archiving, regulation,
monitoring programmes, intercomparison projects, coupled model development, scientist-manager
co-production. (B051,B054,B055,B058,B035-B040 named-tools subset,B011,B013,B018,B019 coupling subset)

## Numbered/enumerated priorities [common, ~15/60]
First/Second/Third enumerations; priority lists tied to named expeditions/plans; leverage points
mapped to actor groups; discrepancy-hypotheses converted directly into numbered research directions.
(B021 prospects,B024 recommendations,B025 road-map steps,B030 First/Second priority list,B047,
B048 six leverage points by actor,B049 First/Second/Third)

## Named vehicles [common, ~12/60]
Agenda anchored to existing or proposed community vehicles: intercomparison projects, sensor
stacks, model resolutions, databases, ongoing surveys, cross-jurisdiction strategies.
(B002 convection-permitting models,B003 monitoring,B004 SAR platforms,B007 Monte Carlo practices,
B009 strategy menus,B039 intercomparison vehicles,B058 named MIPs)

## Closing-section conventions [consensus family]
- Summary mirrors Introduction structure point-for-point before the outlook pivot. (B048 verified;
  B046,B049 candidate) [variant]
- The closing fulfils the Introduction's agenda promise literally: promised gaps delivered
  itemized and matching roadmap items. (B015,B017,B020,B056 + batch-6 verifications) [variant-to-common]
- Case-to-class generalisation move: single-basin/single-site lessons framed as shared by the
  global class, with named analogues. (B041 "might also apply elsewhere",B049) [variant]
- Region-keyed priorities: adaptation/research priority lists for specific regions first.
  (B002 hottest regions first,B007 hotspot tasks) [variant]

## Specificity calibration [dominant mode vs minority variant]
- Dominant mode: mid-level capabilities (build X network, standardize Y protocol, develop Z model
  class) rather than falsifiable hypotheses.
- Minority variant: falsifiable questions / proposed threshold ranges posed explicitly.
  (B025,B026,B027; see gap-identification-patterns.md)
- Time-of-emergence / decision-relevance framing adds urgency to projections-linked items.
  (B039 candidate)

## Register separation [variant-to-common]
Agenda text is deliberately uncited author-judgement register, visually and rhetorically distinct
from the evidence register of body sections. (see citation-patterns.md)

## Imitation risks recorded by miners
- Advocacy tone outrunning evidence if confidence stratification is dropped when copying the form.
  (B024 risk note)
- Normative leverage-point framing without equal evidence depth reads as advocacy. (B048 risk note)
- Insider-framework agendas need external uptake evidence. (B043 risk note)
