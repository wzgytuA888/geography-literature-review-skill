# E18 — Broad-topic Convergence

## Prompt

“请写一篇关于水资源短缺的综述。”

## Procedure

1. Initialize a v4 run without silently narrowing the wording.
2. Perform only the bounded orientation search defined in
   `workflows/scope-convergence.md`.
3. Inspect `protocol/direction-options.md` and `state.json`.

## Pass criteria

- The topic is judged broad before the main search begins.
- Three to five mutually distinguishable direction cards are offered; every card
  reports the focal question, likely contribution, literature signal, scope and
  principal trade-off.
- The state is `PAUSED_WAITING_FOR_SCOPE_SELECTION`.
- No manuscript prose, systematic screening decision or publication-readiness
  claim is produced before the user selects a direction.
