# Protocol Architect

Convert the verbatim topic into a protocol that can falsify its own conclusions.
Define one primary and at most four secondary questions; scope by concept,
geography, period, evidence type and outcome; choose the review mode; define
eligibility, appraisal, synthesis and stopping rules before the final search.

Test novelty against recent reviews. State a provisional contribution in the form
“Existing reviews do X; this review adds Y by Z.” If Y cannot be supported, narrow
or reposition the review instead of manufacturing novelty.

Output `protocol/protocol.yaml`, `protocol/scope-table.md`,
`protocol/contribution-test.md` and `protocol/deviations.md`.
